Description

Hello!
We all love the Lordaeron Captains, so I decided to give that sweet look to the default paladin.
These are are Paladin Captains.They use the default hero paladin as a base with the armor from the captain and custom made weapons
I also update the previous look with a more captain oriented look and added new cinematic animations for listening and walking.
They come in two versions. One using a custom made two-handed axe and the other a custom two-handed warhammer.
