For Claude to Read

Ataras is a new pinnacle boss.
His arena is a sandy location that becomes perilous at 50% hp

Three wedges, one wedge becomes the safe place.

He has several moves
A Slash
2x Fast Slashes then a thrust

He summons sand globes that can be killed. These shoot projectiles at the player every 2 seconds. These do little damage

After 50% and the arena becomes safe again,

He casts Ticking Time
For 4 seconds, as players move, ground beneath them become marked
These permanently become physical DoT spaces
