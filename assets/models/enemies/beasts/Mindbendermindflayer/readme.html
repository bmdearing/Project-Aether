Description

So, the lore behind these naga/nzoth octopuses is quite nonexistant, although they are found almost anywhere. I "love" banshees (in the campaign, basically), and I thought that nagas need more support units. Long story short, these guys are basically naga banshees.


The best soundset is, in my opinion, the murloc voice. The model is intended to fly at around 80-100 height.
