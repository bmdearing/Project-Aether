Description

Yup I've been absent for too long, but here I am with a new toy:
Made the model during the winter so named her Winter Mage.
Was looking for a model that would remind me of DotA2's Drow Ranger's Arcana (since I failed to obtain it...) but I didn't want to make another ranger model so I chose her to be a mage instead, and here is the result hope you guys like it.

Edit: She could also be used as an alternative for DotA Crystal Maiden as well.

_________________________________________________________________


Update:

Two new variants, the first one: with some mesh changes, new animations ( also tried to fix some of those stiff animes, specially during the attack animations ), some jiggly movements ( UHM you know what I mean ) and other changes to the portrait, all thanks to Greedy Martyrdom (the portrait Chad) and an Icon (Winter Mage) beautifully designed by BaiyuGalan (Thank you!), which had me thinking on creating an additional variant (the second one) led me to change her skin tune, her lips, necklace position and some other meshes so that it would resonate better with the icon.
I also kept the older version, since some ppl may prefer the older version.

Hope you guys like The Updated Variants.
