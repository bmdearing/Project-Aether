Hello!
Found some small issues on some of the bandit units.
Also made some small changes to some of them regarding the look.

  • Bandit Lord: Issues with the chains that collided with the skin and shield geoset missing.
    • Changed the axe he was using a bit because it looked dumb
  • Bandit Rouge: Added a version which hold the shield on his back and uses 2 axes (did not add a left hand attack yet...maybe in the future)
    • Added new custom walk 1 and walk defend 1
  • Bandit Enforcer: Issues with the tongue (was not in the correct location for the portrait)
  • Bandit Assasin: Small armor clipping/collisions and made the sword a little bigger
  • Bandit Brigand: Gave him a backpack with spears and sword from the assassin
Hope you enjoy!
