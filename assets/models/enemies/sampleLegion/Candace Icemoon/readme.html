Description

Candace is an Exarch and founder of the Icecrown Exarchate, who had fled Elysium before Sonora's regime. She founded a colony in the Northern Regions, just to unify the Frost Elves, Tuskars, Magnataurs, and Wendigos alike. She is also an old friend of Iyanda Frostrunner, the mother of Revassa Frostrunner that they fought together against the Orcs back in Elysium.
