Description

Hello!
These models comes as a follow up to the original works.
These are part of my human troops that use the captain as a base and are close to the default human soldiers.
They can be used for switch a unit into 3 different versions depending on the weapons you want them to use.

Units composition:

  • Captains:
    • Veteran Captain (sword & shield)
    • Dual-Wield Veteran Captain (two swords)
    • Veteran Spear Captain (spear & shield)
  • Soldiers:
    • Veteran Soldier (sword & shield)
    • Dual-Wield Veteran Soldier (two swords)
    • Veteran Spear Soldier (spear & shield)
Future plans:
  • Archers
  • Battle Casters
  • Knights
Additional information:
  • These units have a lot of custom work added to them, even the original sword&shield unit.
  • You will see while using them that they can be used in a lot of cinematics due to the multiple options for movements.
