Description

I used Zorrot's DE2HD tool to convert the DE models of Inventory Items to serve as the base for the HD models.

Then I used ReterasModelStudio to do the copy-paste work.

Following DE's approach:
I took the helmet, chest, boots, gloves, main weapon, and offhand from Footman.

To avoid introducing new textures:
I took the ring from ArcaneSanctum.
I took the trinket from HeroArchMage.

HD doesn't have the particle effect files that DE has, so you'll need to import itemraritytg1~5.pkb and four particle effect textures to make the item particle effects work.

Don't forget to credit the author.
